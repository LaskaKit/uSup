# LaskaKit μŠup 4x for DIP adapter

An I2C bus hub with μŠup connectors. Connect it to your controller board with one μŠup cable and plug up to three more μŠup, Qwiic or STEMMA QT sensors or modules into the remaining connectors. One more device can be wired through the 2.54 mm pin header. All connectors are wired in parallel, so every device on the bus needs a unique I2C address.

## Specifications

- **Connectors:** 4× μŠup (JST-SH 4-pin, 1 mm pitch, locking)
- **Pin header:** 4 pins, 2.54 mm pitch – SDA, SCL, GND, VCC
- **μŠup pinout:** 1 – GND, 2 – VCC (3.3 V), 3 – SDA, 4 – SCL
- **Compatibility:** μŠup, SparkFun Qwiic, Adafruit STEMMA QT
- **PCB size:** 17.8 × 15.2 × 1.6 mm
- **Mounting holes:** 2× Ø 2.5 mm
